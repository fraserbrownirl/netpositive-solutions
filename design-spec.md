# Design Specification

## Domain Classification
- Primary: government-policy
- Secondary: business-financial
- Rationale: The site translates forest landscape restoration finance into an accessible public-interest briefing while making the structuring and stakeholder implications legible to governments, investors, NGOs, and local actors.

## Design Direction
- Style: Editorial policy briefing with a field-notebook sensibility.
- Mood: Grounded, clear-eyed, quietly optimistic.

## Color System
- Background: #F5F4EE
- Ink: #10221E
- Forest: #154C3B
- Moss: #7D9A70
- Ochre accent: #C78B43
- Rule: #D7DDD4

## Typography
- Display: Georgia / editorial serif for claims and narrative.
- Body: Arial / accessible humanist sans-serif.
- Data: ui-monospace for figures and stage labels.

## Layout Structure
1. Compact global nav and evidence status bar.
2. Hero: thesis, primary CTA, atmospheric restoration image.
3. Evidence-led metrics.
4. Fraser Brown / 2017 pivot.
5. Three-stage capital stack.
6. Stakeholder map.
7. Mechanism matrix.
8. Research shelf.
9. Closing invitation to contribute.
